# 🚀 Lincoln Waste Solutions CRM - User Manual

Welcome to your CRM! This guide is designed to help you understand how each part of the application works in simple, everyday language. Whether you're tracking a new lead or managing a big deal, this manual will show you the way.

## 🧭 Navigation: Understanding Your Tabs

Your sidebar is your command center. Here’s what each tab does:

### 📊 1. Dashboard
**Your "At-a-Glance" View.**
Think of this as your morning coffee page. It gives you a quick summary of:
- How many leads you have in total.
- The value of your potential deals (Pipeline Value).
- A quick list of recent things that happened in the app.

### 🎯 2. Leads
**Your Main Database.**
This is where every potential customer starts. 
- **What to do here:** Add new people you want to work with, edit their contact info, or upload a list of names.
- **Layman's terms:** This is your big virtual Rolodex of everyone you might want to call or email.

### ✅ 3. Qualified Leads
**The "High-Priority" List.**
Not every lead is a perfect fit. When a lead is "Qualified," it means they meet your criteria (like being in the right industry or having the right company size).
- **Special Features:** 
  - **Fetch Leads:** Automatically brings in new leads from our external sources.
  - **Verify Leads:** Checks if their email addresses are real so your emails don't bounce.
- **Layman's terms:** These are the people most likely to say "Yes!"

### 📧 4. Outreach
**The "Messaging Center."**
This is where you start conversations. You don't just send one email; you start a "Sequence."
- **How it works:** You pick a lead, choose a pre-written set of emails, and the app helps manage sending them out.
- **Layman's terms:** This is where you actually reach out and say hello to your leads.

### 🔁 5. Follow-ups
**Managing the "Maybe" Pile.**
Most deals aren't closed on the first email. This tab helps you keep track of people you've already contacted but are still waiting to hear back from or who need a second nudge.
- **Layman's terms:** This is your "Don't forget about these people" list.

### 💬 6. Responses
**The "Inbox."**
When a lead finally replies to your email, they appear here.
- **What to do here:** Read what they said and decide on your next move.
- **Layman's terms:** A dedicated place to see everyone who actually wrote back to you.

### 📅 7. Bookings
**The "Meeting Room."**
Once a lead says, "Let's hop on a call," and a meeting is scheduled, they move here.
- **Layman's terms:** This is your calendar of confirmed appointments and "hot" prospects.

### 🏢 8. Companies
**The "Business Directory."**
Sometimes you're tracking a company rather than just one person. This tab lists all the businesses in your system.
- **Layman's terms:** A list of all the organizations you are currently targeting.

### 🚫 9. DNC List (Do Not Contact)
**The "Safe Zone."**
If someone says "Stop emailing me" or if a company is already a client and shouldn't get sales emails, you put them here.
- **How it works:** The app will automatically block you from sending outreach to anyone on this list.
- **Layman's terms:** Your "Blacklist" to make sure you don't annoy people.

### 👥 10. Contacts
**The "Phone Book."**
A simple list of every individual person across all companies in your CRM.
- **Layman's terms:** A master list of every single person's name and email in the system.

### 📈 11. Pipeline
**The "Big Picture."**
This shows the journey of your deals from "New" to "Closed." It helps you see how much money is potentially coming in.
- **Also includes:** Settings for your email templates (Sequences) and your booking links.
- **Layman's terms:** Your scoreboard. It shows where your money is and how close you are to winning.

## 🛠️ Typical Workflow: How to Use the App

If you're not sure where to start, follow these three simple steps:

1.  **Step 1: Get Leads** 
    - Go to the **Qualified Leads** tab and click **Fetch Leads**.
    - Click **Verify Leads** to make sure the emails are good.
2.  **Step 2: Start Talking**
    - Go to the **Outreach** tab.
    - Pick the people you want to contact and click **Start Sequence**.
3.  **Step 3: Book a Call**
    - Check the **Responses** tab daily.
    - When someone is interested, reply to them and move them to **Bookings** once a meeting is set.

## 💡 Quick Tips for Success
- **Keep it Clean:** If a contact is no longer valid, move them to the **DNC List** so you don't waste time.
- **Check the Dashboard:** Look at your Dashboard every morning to see if you have new replies or upcoming bookings.
- **Email is Key:** The app works best when leads have a verified email address. Always try to fill that in first!
